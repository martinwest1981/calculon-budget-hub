## About Calculon

Calculon handles project budgets, and it is aimed at people whose finances look more like freelance or side gig work than a conventional company setup. The emphasis is on budget management you can actually read, so tracking project costs does not turn into a fight with a crowded interface.

Because it runs in the browser and supports offline operation, Calculon suits anyone who wants their numbers available locally without a permanent connection. For small projects, freelance tasks, or other side-project planning, it offers a lightweight budgeting workspace without unnecessary complexity.
